The Workshop

Story as clinical tool

What it’s about

Storytelling is no longer optional in mental health. It’s an implementation science tool, a stigma-reduction lever, and a way to humanize policy, care, and research. But done wrong, it can do harm.

This workshop takes participants behind the scenes of 100+ films Kay has produced with patients, clinicians, peer specialists, and community members. The focus: trauma-informed production — from recruitment to release.

“Consent isn’t a form. It’s a practice.”

Designed for clinicians, comms teams, program leads, and researchers who want to use media ethically and effectively.
