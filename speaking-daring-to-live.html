The Talk

Living — and leading — on purpose

What it’s about

Kay lived and led from a van for years — a deliberate experiment in what happens when you strip leadership and life down to essentials. This keynote unpacks what the road taught them about authenticity, burnout, courage, and designing a life that actually fits.

It’s a talk about questioning the default script — the one that says leaders have to look, live, and work a certain way — and choosing a different path, whether or not that path involves a van.

“Default isn’t destiny. You’re allowed to design.”

Resonates with leaders, creatives, founders, and anyone feeling the pull to live differently.
