The Talk

One keynote, adapted for your audience

What it’s about

In a world where shame and stigma around mental health can secretly suffocate success and happiness, changing the narrative is key. When vulnerability and adversity are seen as an opportunity to expand, they create a ripple effect in organizations rooted in trust and authenticity.

Using the “crayon box method,” Kay takes audiences through powerful lessons that inspire us to think differently about mental health. They use crayons as a creative and fun metaphor to dig deep and think “outside the box.”

“This keynote is adapted for your audience.”

Kay speaks across Fortune 500, school district leaders, universities, healthcare systems, and nonprofits.
